Welcome to the first FreeMod SMST Tournament!
Also make sure to have joined the discord server
To sign up to 1v1, answer with 1v1
To sign up to 2v2, answer with
Teammate: Player y
Team name : Team z
To usign, edit your forum post



This is an osu!standard 1v1 and 2v2 tournament for people in the rank range of 50k-100k!
If you are top 80 in the SMST rankings, you can only signup to the rank bracket above (ie. you're top 80 in the SMST rankings and are ranked 150k, you can only signup in the 50K-100K bracket)
This will be a fast tournament over in a few hours for both brackets at different dates.
1v1 brackets will have unlimited players.
2v2 brackets will have 32 teams consisting of 2 members each team with no sub players on their team
1v1 brackets will be seeded by the SMST league ranking
Players that are not listed will be considered as having a seed of 0

1v1 will be using Head2Head scorev2.
2v2 will be using TeamVS scorev2.
This tourney is intended to have:
1v1 brackets: RO32 (BO5), RO16(BO5), QuaterFinals(BO5), Semi-Finals(BO7), Finals(BO7)
2v2 brackets: RO16 (BO5), QuarterFinals(BO5), Semi-Finals(BO7), Finals(BO7)
Players/Teams will self-ref in RO32 and RO16 and Quarters. Semi-Finals and Grand Finals will be streamed and referee'd.
This tourney's mappool will consists of 11 freemods, 1TB all in the range of 4.4* - 5*
The mappool will be the same for both brackets.
Mappool should be released on the Sunday before the matches
No-Showing the tournament in two different months will result in a ban from the tournament.

There will be no warmups at RO32, RO16, QF.
Only 1 ban per player/team.
If your match is not posted to #match-results that match is voided
and you will need to redo the match.
You are allowed to pick any mods except NF, Auto and Relax and Movie
Ask your questions on the #questions channel on the discord server for more questions about matches.

1v1 has unlimited players so join all you want

A 2k extra rank range has been allowed as a buffer for anyone who wishes to overrank. Do NOT rank up further than 48k. You have been warned.

Note: Any other info such as procedures and announcements will be on the discord server so be sure to join
Registrations will end for both brackets at 23:59 October 16th(Friday UTC+0).
Bracket Schedules:
1v1 Will start Saturday October 17th 15UTC and should end by 20UTC on the same day
2v2 Will start Sunday October 18th 15UTC and should end by 20UTC on the same day


Feel free to donate !
